Mental-maths shortcut library

Learn a technique, then practise that operation in the Practice tab.

Multiply by 5
Multiply by 10, then divide by 2. Example: 48 × 5 = 480 ÷ 2 = 240.
Multiply by 9
Multiply by 10, then subtract the number. Example: 37 × 9 = 370 − 37 = 333.
Square a number ending in 5
For 35², calculate 3 × 4 = 12 and append 25 → 1225.
Percentage basics
10% means divide by 10; 5% is half of 10%; 1% is divide by 100. So 15% of 240 = 24 + 12 = 36.
Multiply by 25
Multiply by 100, then divide by 4. Example: 36 × 25 = 3600 ÷ 4 = 900.
Multiply by 11 (two-digit number)
For 32 × 11, add 3 + 2 = 5 and place it between the digits → 352. If the sum is 10 or more, carry.
Near 100
For 98 × 97: deficits are 2 and 3. Left part: 98 − 3 = 95. Right part: 2 × 3 = 06. Answer: 9506.
